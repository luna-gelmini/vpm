questkit

dev.ladyluh.questkit · v1.0.0

Quest Planner. Looks at a PC avatar, picks the best Quest version of every material, texture, PhysBone and mesh, says why, and builds a separate Quest copy you can tweak.

ps1ify

dev.ladyluh.ps1ify · v1.0.0 · needs questkit

Turns an avatar into a cute PS1-style one: low-poly, baked facets, dithered pixel textures. Builds a Quest and a PC version and keeps the face's expressions.

dev.ladyluh.source2vrchat · v1.0.0

Unity half of source2vrchat. Sets up Garry's Mod and Source Filmmaker models converted with gma2blend: humanoid rig, materials, eye look and helper bone constraints.
